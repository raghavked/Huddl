# Operating Agreement of uHearth LLC

> **Status: parked. The operator chose the sole proprietor route for
> launch (August 2026), so no LLC exists and nothing signs this yet. It
> stays in the repo because the analysis in COMPLIANCE.md recommends
> revisiting the entity question at real scale, and this document is ready
> the day uHearth LLC is formed.**
>
> **Template, drafted by a machine, not yet reviewed by a lawyer.**
> A single-member operating agreement in plain language. States including
> California require one to exist; none of them file it. Fill the bracketed
> values after the Articles of Organization are approved, print it, sign it,
> and keep it with the company records. Have an attorney read it before the
> company signs anything bigger than a SaaS subscription.

This Operating Agreement of uHearth LLC (the "Company") is adopted effective
[DATE] by its sole member, [MEMBER NAME] (the "Member").

## 1. Formation

The Company was formed as a limited liability company under the laws of
[STATE] by the filing of Articles of Organization with the [STATE] Secretary
of State on [DATE]. The rights and duties of the Member are as set out in
this agreement and, where this agreement is silent, in [STATE]'s limited
liability company act.

## 2. Name and purpose

The Company's name is uHearth LLC. It may do business as "Hearth" and under
the domain uhearth.app. Its purpose is to build and operate software,
including the Hearth campus community application, and to do anything else
lawful for a limited liability company in [STATE].

## 3. Principal office

The Company's principal office is [ADDRESS], or wherever the Member later
designates.

## 4. Sole member

The Member owns one hundred percent of the membership interest in the
Company. No additional members may be admitted except by a written amendment
to this agreement signed by the Member.

## 5. Management

The Company is managed by its Member. The Member has full authority to act
for the Company: to open and operate bank accounts, enter contracts, hire
help, register intellectual property, and settle disputes. No officer or
manager exists unless the Member appoints one in writing.

## 6. Capital and property

The Member's initial contribution is recorded in the Company's records. The
Company's property belongs to the Company, not to the Member personally.
That includes the uhearth.app domain, the Hearth codebase and repository,
the trademarks and branding, and the accounts that run the service (hosting,
email, developer program memberships), each of which shall be held in or
transferred to the Company's name.

## 7. Separateness

The Company keeps its own bank account, and Company money and the Member's
personal money do not mix. The Company pays its own expenses from its own
account; the Member takes money out only as recorded distributions or
recorded reimbursements. The Company signs in its own name, with the Member
signing as "Member, uHearth LLC" rather than personally. This section exists
because the Company's limited liability depends on it being observed.

## 8. Distributions

The Company may distribute cash or property to the Member at the Member's
discretion, except that no distribution may be made if, after it, the
Company could not pay its debts as they come due.

## 9. Taxes

As a single-member limited liability company, the Company is disregarded as
an entity separate from its Member for federal income tax purposes unless
the Member elects otherwise. Its activity is reported on the Member's
returns. The Company obtains and uses its own employer identification
number.

## 10. Liability and indemnification

The Member is not personally liable for the debts or obligations of the
Company solely by being its Member. The Company shall indemnify the Member
for acts taken in good faith on the Company's behalf, to the fullest extent
[STATE] law allows, but not for acts of fraud, willful misconduct, or a
knowing violation of law.

## 11. Books and records

The Company keeps at its principal office: this agreement and any
amendments, the Articles of Organization, records of contributions and
distributions, tax filings, and ordinary financial records.

## 12. Transfer

The Member may transfer all or part of the membership interest by written
assignment. On a transfer of the whole interest, the transferee becomes the
Member on signing a counterpart of this agreement.

## 13. Dissolution

The Company dissolves when the Member decides in writing to dissolve it, or
when [STATE] law requires. On dissolution, the Company pays or provides for
its debts, then distributes what remains to the Member, then files the
certificates [STATE] requires.

## 14. Amendment and entire agreement

This agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by the Member. It is
the entire agreement about the Company's internal affairs and replaces any
earlier understanding.

## 15. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of [STATE].
